Canada's Real-Time Rail launched on November 9 as a 24/7 instant payment system, and no Big Six bank was in the launch cohort. The eight initial participants were Wealthsimple (the largest Canadian share), KOHO, Questbank, Peoples Trust, and a handful of digital-first players. Settlement speed is now table stakes, and the incumbents ceded the first move to a neobank.

The rail is the third piece of Wealthsimple's stack. Penny is the AI assistant. CADD is the CAD-backed stablecoin. The RTR is the payment rail. One company now controls the interface, the settlement asset, and the payment infrastructure for a growing share of Canadian depositors, while the Big Six are still blocking external agents and building internal tools.

The same week, the European Banking Association's Open Finance Working Group published "The Agent-Ready Bank," defining the terms of engagement for a world where the customer's entry point is an independent AI agent querying providers across an open market. It names the threat directly: banks reduced to commoditized backends, a "balance-sheet utility" risk. It also defines the solution: the agent-facing trust layer of agent authentication, liability and consent for non-human actors, and agent-facing interfaces. EBA standards reach the EU in roughly 18 to 24 months; Canadian adoption follows.

The US is moving faster on the regulatory side. The OCC issued a supervisory framework for bank use of generative and agentic AI, with four risk tiers, six pillars, and controls that scale with the consequences of agent failure. Canada is about 12 months behind. OSFI's Routledge is developing an AI safety code with consultations starting next spring.

The architecture is converging from three directions: a retail rail that commoditizes settlement speed, a wholesale rail that commoditizes the settlement asset, and a standard that defines the trust layer for non-human actors. When all three are in place, what remains strategically valuable is the trust infrastructure that makes agent-mediated banking safe: the deterministic layer with governed boundaries. National Bank's CEO named the condition and the strategy in October. The December earnings call will show whether monitoring becomes building.
