Three competing card networks aligned on agent identity for the first time. Visa, Mastercard, and Ant International launched cross-network Know-Your-Agent interoperability. The projected market is $3-5 trillion in agentic commerce by 2030. Banks not at the KYA table are on the menu.

Ant International did not stop there. It launched a full-stack AI-native financial suite — 100 products, including bank accounts designed specifically for AI agents. "Account for Agent" comes with KYA smart contracts and account-to-account nano-settlement at a fraction of a cent per transaction. Ant's proprietary payment foundation models are already deployed at Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank, and Standard Chartered. When the customer is an agent, the product IS the API.

Chime acquired Stride Bank for $590 million. The 10.4-million-member neobank bought a national charter, projecting $100 million-plus in synergies. This is concurrent with Revolut and Nubank pursuing their own charter moves. The signal is clear: when products commoditize, the license IS the moat. The regulatory permission to hold deposits and lend is irreducible and non-commoditizable.

National Bank of Canada partnered with Sardine's agentic risk platform, with NAventures leading a $25 million Series C. BNC is deploying agentic AI across retail, commercial, and wealth for fraud and financial crime detection. The equity stake signals category investment, not just a vendor purchase. Fraud and compliance is the most defensible near-term agentic AI territory.

Mastercard and Backbase moved agentic banking into production for 120+ banks across 50 countries. Column launched unified stablecoin-card-banking infrastructure, already moving billions for Ramp, Brex, and Mercury — bypassing traditional bank intermediation entirely. Canada's Real-Time Rail by-law took effect August 24, Q4 2026 launch confirmed.

The IMF published its three-layer payments framework this week, calling for KYA standards and flagging the legal liability gap in agent-mediated transactions.

Three defensible layers are emerging. First, the charter — regulatory permission to hold deposits and lend. Chime's $590M confirms its value. Second, trust infrastructure for agent-mediated transactions — KYA verification, agent monitoring, settlement finality. BNC's Sardine deployment is a concrete step into this territory. Third, data asymmetry — the bank sees behavioral signals, dispute history, and fraud scores no external agent can read via API.

What is eroding: payment intermediation, as Column bypasses it. The customer interface, as agents mediate it. Product differentiation, as 120+ banks run the same Backbase stack. The moats are narrowing to the irreducible.
