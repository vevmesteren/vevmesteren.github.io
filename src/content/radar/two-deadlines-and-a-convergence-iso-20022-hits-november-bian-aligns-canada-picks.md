Two deadlines and a convergence shaped this week in banking reference models.

Swift's ISO 20022 structured-address cutover was set for November 2026. In August, Swift extended the deadline after uneven industry progress, but a December 2026 update is expected and the cutover is still coming, More than 98% of payment instructions now use ISO 20022 format. Banks that treat the extension as cancellation will fall behind on data quality. The G20 cross-border payments progress report, published November 16, confirms ISO 20022 data quality as central to reducing cost and improving transparency.

Canada's Consumer-Driven Banking framework moved from legislation to implementation. Bill C-15 received Royal Assent in March 2026. Regulations were pre-published in Canada Gazette Part I in June 2026. Payments Canada submitted detailed recommendations on October 26, 2026, calling for a sound regulatory foundation for the initial "read" access phase before moving to "write" access and payment initiation. The Bank of Canada published an implementation discussion note in May 2026 outlining deliverables and dependencies. The next decision point is the designation of the Technical Standards Body, which will choose the API standard Canadian banks must adopt. FDX is the likely foundation.

FDX launched an agentic AI and open finance initiative on April 14, 2026, with a Call for Input on how AI agents should share financial data under technical standards. FDX API version 4.5 was released, expanding open finance scope. The standard is moving beyond banking into broader financial data sharing, and is preparing for AI-agent-mediated access.

BIAN released Service Landscape version 14.0 on March 13, 2026. The update adds closer mapping to ISO 20022 message definitions, semantic APIs, and 340 service domains. The tighter BIAN-to-ISO 20022 alignment reduces friction between internal banking architecture and external payment messaging. BIAN v14.0 positions itself as the internal reference model that bridges external standards.

Globally, open finance is reaching production scale. Brazil reported 269 million active consents as of September 2026, Australia's Consumer Data Right saw record opt-in levels. The EU's FIDA framework is advancing through the legislative process. Canada is a late mover but can draw on all of these models.

The strategic signal: adopt BIAN v14.0 as the internal capability map, FDX as the external open banking API standard, and ISO 20022 as the payment messaging standard. Invest in the mappings between them.
