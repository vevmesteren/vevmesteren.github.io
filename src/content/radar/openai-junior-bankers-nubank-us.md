OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Financial Services this week, co-designed with Morgan Stanley and Evercore to automate the exact work junior analysts do: comp analysis, LBO modeling, pitchbook preparation. GPT-6 Astra ships with these capabilities baked in. Morgan Stanley co-designed for roadmap influence; every competitor gets the same tool. Gartner reports 41% of banks are planning agent deployments within twelve months. The apprenticeship pipeline risk (who trains the next generation of senior bankers when the junior work is automated) remains unaddressed.

Nubank entered the United States. The world's largest neobank, 140 million customers, brought stablecoin-backed yields to mainstream deposits: 3.5% APY, 1.5% unlimited cashback, fee-free transfers across 35+ countries. A sponsor-bank partnership with Lead Bank provides FDIC coverage; an OCC charter is pending for 2027. Crypto-native yield infrastructure is now entering mainstream US consumer banking. Deposit competition has a new dimension: yield source.

NPCI and NVIDIA unveiled the first open reinforcement learning training environment for banking AI agents. Built on NVIDIA NeMo, fully synthetic (no real customer data) and MCP-compatible. Designed for NeMo Gym contribution. This is the first open-source agent benchmarking infrastructure at national payments scale, serving 1.4 billion people. It sets a precedent for how banks train and evaluate agents without touching production data.

Gartner named its "Coolest Vendors" in Agentic AI for Banking. Expert.ai leads with a neurosymbolic approach delivering 90% AML false-positive reduction and 70% KYC time savings. Ryt Bank published the first peer-reviewed paper on a regulator-approved AI-native bank architecture: a four-agent system with guardrails, intent, payment, and FAQ layers, serving 1.5 million users in Malaysia.

Swift's Digital Ledger completed its second live weekend cross-border settlement. DBS and Citi settled USD in minutes on a Saturday. Tokenized deposits (not stablecoins) preserve the bank balance sheet and can pay interest. BNP Paribas and HSBC completed the first corporate treasury payment for Siemens. Ripple deployed agentic AI in a billion-dollar corporate treasury: the first autonomous treasury agent that acts, not just recommends.
