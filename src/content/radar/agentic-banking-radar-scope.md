Every Monday morning, a scheduled job scans the open web for signals where AI agents start acting in banking. Not what banks say about AI. What they do when agents show up.

The radar watches five search areas: agentic banking and AI-native bank architecture, fintech competitive moves and neobank innovation, payments and rail modernization, customer trust and deposit competition, and regulatory shifts. Each finding gets mapped to BIAN service domains, assessed through six strategic lenses, and tagged by signal strength: new, strengthening, or fading.

The lenses are the point. Six questions that turn news into strategy:

- **Commoditization**: what happens when banking products become commodities an agent can swap?
- **Maturity model**: where is the market on the curve from digitized to agentic?
- **Strategic value**: what remains valuable if the customer's primary interface is an agent the bank doesn't control?
- **Trust**: how do you maintain a trust relationship when banking is intermediated?
- **Maturity assessment**: how do we measure where a bank, a customer, or a market actually is?
- **Deposits strategy**: does this grow deposits or leak them?

The radar runs because reading about this isn't enough. Building exposes what's real. The scan catches the signals that tell you which building is worth doing.
