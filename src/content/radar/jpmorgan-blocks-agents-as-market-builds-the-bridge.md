Jamie Dimon drew the line on the Q3 call: "not going to allow just any outside agent to come in." JPMorgan blocked Muse and Instinct from customer accounts, permitting read-only access via Plaid while shutting down agent-initiated money movement. CFO Jeremy Barnum warned of liability transfer and said agents "could intensify competition for deposits." The defensive template is JPMorgan's own Smart Cash agent, built inside the bank.

The market built the bridge in two days. On October 15, Self Labs launched Agent Vault, a zero-knowledge credential layer that lets agents sign in and pay without ever seeing the credentials — connecting to Muse, Claude, Codex, Grok, and Hermes at nineteen dollars a month. On October 8, Crossmint shipped the Agent Commerce Toolkit: a single API combining Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay, a card vault, and merchant checkout. Agents can now pay with any card at any store through one integration. The agent payment layer is fully commoditized, and it is entirely non-bank.

Meanwhile, TD extended agentic AI across the full mortgage lifecycle — pre-adjudication, funding, and discharge — building on the May pilot that compressed fifteen hours to three minutes. Human-in-the-loop throughout: AI assembles and flags, humans decide, designed to double productivity in select processes. TD is now the Canadian benchmark for agentic mortgage operations.

The settlement-unit battle opened in earnest. A G-SIB and a tech firm completed a Hong Kong pilot using tokenized bank deposits — not stablecoins — on the Jovay L2 for AI agent micropayments under two dollars, settled in real time via MCP. Banks and stablecoin issuers are now competing to be the settlement unit of the AI economy. OSFI's stance on federally regulated institutions issuing tokenized deposits will decide whether Canadian banks are providers or bystanders.

Why it matters: the North Star question went from theory to production this week. The agent payment stack has three layers — card networks, protocols, tooling — and banks are in none of them. The deposit is the only remaining touchpoint. Strategic value is shifting to the settlement unit, agent identity and delegation infrastructure, and governed agent connectivity. The liability framework is still the gap, and the bank that fills it becomes indispensable.
